TRANSPARENT SCORING

How the matchup model works

This is a deterministic heuristic for sorting a matchup—not an official VS Battles Wiki rating, a battle simulator, or empirical win-rate prediction. It uses profile-based qualitative estimates because fictional feats and cross-verse scaling do not reduce cleanly to one number.

Context and safeguards

  • Profiles keep the wiki's displayed tier and stat wording; a separate 0–10 rating is only an app-side comparison aid.
  • Standard equipment is included. Preparation boosts apply only when switched on; home-field boosts only apply when that arena is selected.
  • Elemental bonuses require a documented attack, weakness, or resistance match. Unknown interactions stay neutral.
  • Hax is estimated for this particular opponent and setting. The model cannot establish whether a specific ability bypasses a specific resistance unless the profile data says so.
  • Use source links on the profile cards to audit the text. Improve accuracy by editing the profile ratings and evidence notes as you add verified feats.
The displayed edge percentage is a scaled visualization of the score margin, not a calibrated probability that the character wins.
